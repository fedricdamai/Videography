## Checks

- [ ] One spine, one world, one hero object or material, one accent, one display face.
- [ ] Every move is native to the world.
- [ ] Every move serves a beat or the hero object; nothing is decoration.
- [ ] Sync is chosen once and never switches.
- [ ] At most one hybrid seam, crossed once at a clear beat.
- [ ] Length fits the category: new means explain (75-125s), familiar means show (20-40s).
- [ ] The CTA matches the goal.
