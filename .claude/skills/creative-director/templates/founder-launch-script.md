# Founder-led launch script

Fill every bracket. Target 280-320 words, read at 155-180 words per minute: 95-125 seconds. Each line gets its own visual. The `VISUAL` column is filled from the noun list (every noun and number in the line).

Evidence: `docs/launch-video-formula.md`.

| # | Beat | Line (fill in) | Speaker | VISUAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Claim (0-6s) | "[We raised $X / Introducing Product], the first [category] that lets you [specific outcome] [without the usual pain]." | Founder on camera, name and title lower third | Founder A-roll |
| 1b | Optional cold open (max 17s) | A 3-line skit where the viewer's problem happens to the founder, ending "That wasn't real, but you get the point." | Founder | Skit |
| 2 | Stakes (optional) | "[Proof number] has already [happened]. And now [bigger future]." | VO | Rolling number |
| 3 | Pivot | "Let me show you how it works." | Founder on camera | A-roll |
| 4 | Persona | "Say you're a [specific business] in [place], and you [specific situation]." | VO | Persona metaphor in the brand material |
| 5 | Feature 1 | "In [time], you can [action] [with named brands / number]." | VO | Real UI, zoomed, cursor |
| 6 | Feature 2 | "[Action] via [named rails / tools]." | VO | Real UI |
| 7 | Feature 3 | "You can even [surprising capability]." | VO | Metaphor object |
| 8 | How it's possible | "[Mechanism] is [backed by / powered by] [credible thing]." | VO | Object + labeled chips with lines |
| 9 | Enemy | "[Incumbents / the industry] [three concrete sins, specific verbs]." | VO | Dark treatment, bleeped names, their pain shown |
| 10 | Our promise | "[Us], [our counter-promise]. If we ever [fail], we'll [outrageous, human guarantee]." | Founder on camera | A-roll, then the joke cutaway |
| 11 | Proof stack | "We [volume number]. [Count] [customers] already trust us, backed by [investors]." | VO | Big number, logo wall, investor tiles |
| 12 | Gift | "To celebrate the launch, [we're giving away / sign up in the next N days and get] [gift]." | Founder | Gift as an object (split-flap, spreadsheet, score) |
| 13 | One more thing (optional) | "One last thing: [bonus]. The right [persona] could use this to [huge outcome]." | Founder | Bonus UI |
| 14 | CTA | "[Retweet and] comment [KEYWORD], and we'll DM you [the link / the gift]." | Founder | Social post UI, keyword typed into the reply box |

## Checks before recording

- [ ] The claim names a category, an outcome and a removed pain, in one sentence.
- [ ] "Let me show you how it works." lands by 30% of the runtime.
- [ ] The persona could be photographed: a business, a place, a situation.
- [ ] Every feature line has a number or a named brand.
- [ ] The enemy line uses concrete verbs ("freeze", "nuke", "vanish"), not adjectives.
- [ ] There is exactly one joke.
- [ ] The gift is useful to people who won't buy yet, and is ready to send.
- [ ] The keyword is one word.
- [ ] Word count is 280-320.
