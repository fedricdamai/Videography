## Chimera Wallet

From the live site, [chimerawallet.com](https://chimerawallet.com/) (rendered 27 Nov 2026), unless noted.

- **What it is:** "The only Bitcoin super-app built so nobody can ever take your money away." Non-custodial. Mainchain, Lightning (via Boltz) and Arkade Protocol VTXOs in one app. "First Super App on Arkade Protocol."
- **Proof points on the site:** "Your keys. Nobody in between." Comparison table: custody non-custodial, speed instant, KYC optional, unilateral exit yes.
- **Verbs on the site:** Control, Trade, Earn.
- **Raised:** "$7.7M raised across two rounds."
- **Launch:** v0.1 PWA on 20 Apr 2026 ([Blockonomi](https://blockonomi.com/chimera-wallet-review-2026-non-custodial-pwa-based-is-it-worth-it/), promotional review). **Verify** with the team.
- **Partnership:** Nimbus Capital, "$15 million strategic partnership" to expand DeFi on Bitcoin, 18 Feb 2026 ([GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/02/18/3239958/0/en/Nimbus-Capital-and-Chimera-Wallet-announce-15-million-strategic-partnership-to-expand-DeFi-infrastructure-on-Bitcoin.html)). Conflicts with the site's $7.7M, so it stays off screen.
- **Card:** pre-orders, "1.5% transaction fee locked for life, first 1,000 pre-orders". Issued with Wirex per [Wirex](https://www.wirexapp.com/post/spend-bitcoin-like-you-spend-money-chimera-card-is-coming). Not live yet as far as the site shows.
- **Company:** Chimera Software Association, Lugano, Switzerland. CEXT utility token issued by Outlogic SAGL.
