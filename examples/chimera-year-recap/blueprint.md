# Chimera Wallet: 2026 in 60 seconds

Facts and sources: `research.md`. Method: `/creative-director`, `docs/decision-logic.md`.

## Product truth

Chimera turns "withdrawals paused" into **nothing**: no one in between to pause anything.

- What people put up with: an exchange holding their coins, and a grey button the day it matters.
- Feeling once it's gone: calm.
- The number we can prove: $6.4B left the world's largest exchange in about 20 minutes, and its withdrawals stopped. (Chimera's own numbers are too small to carry a receipts film, so the market supplies them.)
- Audience wants: quiet confidence. It holds savings.

## Three concepts

| | A. Paused | B. The newsroom | C. Noise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sentence | The year told in its own receipts, and every one ends on a grey "Withdraw" button. Except one. | 2026 as a newsroom where the breaking banners never stop, until one wallet isn't news. | Twelve months of headlines crushed into ten seconds, then black, then one phone. |
| Device | **Receipts** | The world reacts | Subtract |
| Hook (0-2s, what we see) | A thumb taps Withdraw. The button greys out: WITHDRAWALS PAUSED. | A red BREAKING banner slams across a trading desk. | A wall of 40 headlines scrolling too fast to read. |
| Last line | "Math doesn't pause withdrawals." | "Some wallets don't make the news." | "Your keys. Nobody in between." |
| Verdict | **Pick.** Every fact is real, the hook is a feeling everyone had in October, and the button is a hero object we can hold. | Loses: needs AI anchors (likeness risk), and staging fake news around real hacks blurs fact and fiction in a film whose power is that it's true. | Loses: subtraction works for one moment, not 60 seconds. Viewers leave without one fact to repeat. Its void survives inside A. |

Competitor test on A: any self-custody wallet could run "not your keys". Two things make it Chimera's only: the April 20 beat plants Chimera's launch inside the timeline, and the payoff is an Arkade exit that any exchange-backed wallet can't claim.

### Transition chain (key sequence 6-38s)

Every cut in the timeline is carried by the date card rolling forward (anchor). Into the stat beats, the pill carries the eye (a peak circled, a number held inside it). Into the void: overload. Out of the void: type. Into the product: the April green dot, planted at 15s, becomes the button. No dead cuts.

## Cut list

- **Coldcard firmware theft (Jul 30, $88.6M to $116M).** True and huge, but it's a self-custody failure. In a film that ends on "your keys", it invites "so are keys safe?" Belongs in a separate security piece.
- **Ledger reseller implant.** Same reason, plus it attacks a named competitor.
- **Naming Binance with its logo.** Legal exposure. "The world's largest exchange" plus a source tag says the same thing. Team decides.
- **Chimera's $15M Nimbus partnership.** Conflicts with the site's $7.7M raised. No unconfirmed number on screen.
- **CEXT token and the card's 1.5% offer.** A token pitch inside a recap reads as a shill. Save the card for its own offer film.
- **"Not a promise. Not a policy. Mathematics."** The brand's line, but its rhythm fights the fade. "Math doesn't pause withdrawals." ties the claim to the hero object.
- **Ethereum Glamsterdam.** Mainnet date unconfirmed, and it isn't a Bitcoin story.
- **AI news anchors or real news footage.** Likeness and licensing risk. Screen-filmed graphics only.
- **Voiceover.** Captions carry it sound-off, and one cut works in every language.
- **Word-per-beat manifesto type, confetti, store badges.** Other worlds.
- **A second joke** ("The CLARITY Act. Not very clear."). One joke, and it's spent on April.
- **Product by 3s rule:** broken on purpose. Chimera is planted at 15s and paid off at 43.5s. Showing it earlier kills the turn.
