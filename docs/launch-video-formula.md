# The Launch Video Formula

How agencies make launch videos that go viral over and over: Shown Media's founder-led films for Slash, Contra and Meridian, and BLVD's motion film for Phantom.

Their trick is a system, not taste. One script skeleton, one distribution engine and one visual rule, re-skinned per brand. Every timing below was measured from the videos, and every script line comes from a transcript of the audio.

## 2. The script skeleton (founder-led)

All three Shown Media scripts follow the same order. The repeated phrases are real: they are lifted from the transcripts.

| # | Beat | When (share of runtime) | Slash | Contra | Meridian |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The claim**: one sentence, credential plus "first [category] that lets you [outcome] [without the pain]" | 0-5% | "We raised $60 million to build the first business banking platform that allows you to operate in the U.S. without an LLC." | "Introducing Contra Payments, the world's first payments platform that allows you to sell to AI agents." | Skit first (17s), then "Every day, billions of decisions are being made by AI. And if AI doesn't say your brand, you're losing sales. Introducing Meridian." |
| 2 | **Stakes** (optional) | 5-15% | | "Over 200 million dollars has already been paid out… and now billions will be paid… by humans and their agents." | |
| 3 | **The pivot** | 5-32% | "Let me show you how it works." | "Let me show you how it works." | "Let me show you how it works." |
| 4 | **The persona**: one specific, picturable customer | right after the pivot | "Say you're a supplement brand based in Dubai, but you sell in the U.S." | "Let's say you're a designer selling an AI brand kit." | "Let's say you're a sneaker company that wants to get more customers from AI search." |
| 5 | **The walkthrough**: 3 to 5 features, one sentence each, each with a number or a named brand | to about 60% | "In 10 minutes… directly from Shopify, PayPal, Amazon, or Stripe" | "In one click… you get paid a thousand dollars instantly from this AI agent." | "Every day, Meridian runs thousands of prompts… gives you these five recommendations" |
| 6 | **The enemy** | 43-67% | "Platforms like [bleeped] and [bleeped] bleed you with fees, freeze your money without warning, nuke your account while you're asleep" | "While other platforms are celebrating eliminating jobs with AI, we are dedicated to getting creatives paid billions by AI." | (competitors who get mentioned by AI instead of you) |
| 7 | **The outrageous promise** | after the enemy | "If your funds ever get wrongfully held for over 48 hours, we'll donate $10,000 to a charity of your choice and send you an apology video from my mom." | | |
| 8 | **Proof stack** | 63-75% | "$10 billion in payments per year. 3,000 businesses… NEA, Y Combinator, and Menlo Ventures." | | |
| 9 | **The launch gift**: urgency or a lead magnet | 72-83% | "Sign up in the next 14 days and we'll give you 4.5% cash rewards" | "A secret spreadsheet with the top 100 digital services and products that AI agents are already looking for" | "We're giving away the fastest way to check your AI search score." |
| 10 | **"The right [person] could…"** | 83-92% | "The right operator could use this agent to save 6 or 7 figures per year." | "The right creatives could build up products for agents and make millions of dollars." | |
| 11 | **Comment-to-get CTA** | 92-95%, last 5 to 8s | "Retweet and comment Slash, and we'll DM you a link." | "Comment Contra and we'll send it to you." | "Comment AI, and I'll send you a link." |

**Pace:** 154 to 180 words per minute, about 290 to 320 words for 95 to 125 seconds. That's fast, and every sentence carries one fact.

## 3. Why it goes viral every time

1. **Comment-to-get is the engine.** Asking for a one-word reply in exchange for a link turns viewers into commenters. On X, replies and reposts push a post to more people, and every reply is a lead you can DM. The video ends by *showing* the reply box with the word typed in, so nobody has to think about what to do.
2. **The gift works even on people who don't need the product.** A secret spreadsheet, a free score or a cash bonus gives everyone a reason to comment. The product doesn't have to be relevant to them yet.
3. **A founder's face buys trust.** A real person, name and title on screen, speaking in first person ("my team spent the last 70 days"). The founder is the guarantee.
4. **An enemy gives the story stakes.** Named or bleeped incumbents, or an industry narrative to stand against. People share takes, not features.
5. **One joke humanises it.** The apology video from the founder's mom. Meridian's ChatGPT skit about apologising to a girlfriend. One laugh per film, never more.
6. **Captions on every line.** Most people watch muted, so the script must work as text.
7. **The skeleton never changes.** Only the art direction and the persona do. That's how an agency ships these on repeat.

## 4. The visual system: say it, see it

Founder-led films have two layers.

**A-roll: the founder.** Medium shot in a real workplace (office, studio, loft). Name and title as a lower third on first appearance. The founder returns on four beats only: the claim, the pivot, the enemy or promise, and the CTA.

**B-roll: everything else, driven by the script.** Every spoken noun gets its own visual within about half a second.

| Spoken | On screen (Slash) |
|---|---|
| "Say you're a supplement brand based in Dubai" | Gold 3D UAE with a pin and a founder avatar |
| "but you sell in the U.S." | Dashed flight path from UAE to the U.S. with the Slash wordmark |
| "ready to receive funds directly from Shopify, PayPal, Amazon, or Stripe" | Real transactions list filling with those exact brand names |
| "like USDC or USDT" | A gold Tether coin drops in |
| "Slash's very own stablecoin, USDSL" | A Slash coin rises out of a black card, and a split-flap board flips to U-S-D-S-L |
| "pegged to the value of the dollar" | The split-flap flips to VALUE = $1 |
| "backed one-to-one by U.S. Treasuries and USDC" | Lines draw from the coin to labeled chips: US Treasuries, Cash equivalents |
| "vanish when you beg for answers" | A phone keypad dialling into nothing |
| "held for over 48 hours" | A gold clock face spinning |
| "Sign up in the next 14 days" | A split-flap calendar flipping to 14 |

The same rule in the other two: Contra's "a payment link that works anywhere" draws an orbit of platform icons around the word "Anywhere". "Any buyer, and yes, even agents" turns an ASCII dot-matrix face into a grid of identical faces. "Millions of agents" becomes a wall of colored pills: Sophie's Agent, Fabian's Agent, Nina's Agent.

**Each brand gets one material.** Slash is gold metal on black. Contra is white paper with saturated color. Meridian is white with orange nodes and threads. Every cutaway is built from that one material, which is why two minutes of cutaways still feel like one film.

**Real UI, close up.** Product demos use the real interface with a cursor, zoomed in so a phone viewer can read it. Never a full-screen desktop at 100%.

**The CTA is shown, not just said.** All three end on a social post UI with the keyword being typed into the reply box.

## 5. Phantom: the motion manifesto

A different format for when there's no founder story to tell. Pure motion design, 48 seconds, no voice.

| Time | What happens | Move |
|---|---|---|
| 0-4s | "how" on black, then MetaMask's real "Connect with MetaMask" windows multiply across the screen | The enemy's own UI as the problem |
| 4-6s | "time to level up" with a burst of purple dashes | Word-per-beat type |
| 6-7s | The word "and" scales up until the camera flies through its letters | Type-through transition |
| 7-8s | MetaMask's orange "switch" pill **morphs** into Phantom's purple pill with the ghost | Competitor morph |
| 8-10s | "upgrade / your / wallet", one word per beat, phone rotating behind | Depth sandwich |
| 10-19s | Phones in circle masks: search, collectibles, "Pin it!" and "Hide it!" sticker labels | Shape rhyme, sticker callouts |
| 20-23s | "stop switching networks", then MetaMask's network dropdown | Enemy again |
| 23-45s | Features as single verbs: Personalize, Reorganize, stay, store, drag, connect, multichain. 3D coins float through | One verb per beat |
| 45-48s | "a wallet you'll love", 3D wallet, then phantom.app | Resolve |

Cuts lock to the music and land at 0.83 of the beat: a little early, the same move Plasma uses. Feature names are verbs the viewer does, not nouns the product has.

## 6. How to make one

### Founder-led launch (90-125s)

1. **Write the script first** with the template in `.claude/skills/creative-director/templates/founder-launch-script.md`. Aim for 280 to 320 words. Read it aloud at speed.
2. **Pick the gift and the keyword** before anything else. Gift: a free tool, a secret list or a time-boxed bonus. Keyword: one word, ideally the brand name.
3. **Choose the material.** One substance every cutaway is made of (gold metal, paper, glass, clay, neon).
4. **Shoot the founder.** One setup, medium shot, real workplace, good mic. Record the whole script 3 times. Shoot a second angle only if there are two founders.
5. **Build the noun list.** Go through the script and list every noun and number. Each one gets a visual:
   - Product nouns: real UI, built with `/stageshow` and animated in `/hyperframes`.
   - Abstract nouns (stablecoin, anywhere, agents): a metaphor object in the brand material. Generate stills with an image model, animate with image-to-video or in HyperFrames.
   - Numbers: rolling values, split-flaps, counters (`examples/stat-slam`, `examples/milestone-chart`).
   - Named brands and incumbents: logos, or a bleep and symbols ("P$%@l") for enemies.
6. **Cut to the voice, not the beat.** These films don't lock to music. A new visual lands within half a second of its noun.
7. **Caption every line**, small, bottom center.
8. **End on the reply box** with the keyword being typed.
9. **Post copy:** the claim sentence as the first line, then "Comment [keyword] and we'll send you [gift]." Have someone ready to send DMs the moment it goes live.

### Motion manifesto (40-50s)

1. Find the enemy's UI moment that every user hates (account switching, network dropdowns, fee screens).
2. Write the film as one line broken into single words, one per beat.
3. Morph the enemy's element into yours.
4. Features as verbs, one per beat.
5. Lock cuts to the grid, a quarter beat early.

## 7. Guardrails

- **Bleep or symbol-mask incumbents' names** if you attack them, as Slash does. Don't show their logos in a negative light without legal review.
- **The gift must be real and deliverable** the minute the post goes live. A broken DM promise burns the audience.
- **Comment-to-get must follow platform rules.** Ask for comments, don't buy them, and send what you promised.
- **No real person's likeness** in skits without written permission. Meridian's skit uses its own presenter.
- **Every number spoken must be defensible**, with a source on file.
