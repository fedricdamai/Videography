# Decision Logic

How the studios behind 14 reference videos picked one coherent film out of dozens of possible styles, and how we do the same.

The short answer: **nobody combines styles.** Each film is the output of three decisions made in order. Goal picks the story. Audience picks the world. The story's beats pick the moves, and only moves native to that world are allowed. What looks like a mix of techniques is always one world used consistently.

## 1. The decisions behind each film

Reconstructed from the films, their post copy and their timing.

| Film | Goal (from the post) | Audience | Is the category new? | Spine (story) | World (style) | Length | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Phantom | Switch users from MetaMask | Crypto users | No | Enemy's pain, morph, verbs | Kinetic manifesto | 48s | URL |

### What the table shows

1. **A new category buys a long, explained film. A familiar one gets a short, shown film.** All three new-category launches (Slash, Contra, Meridian) run 96 to 124s, with a founder explaining a specific customer's case. All the familiar-category launches (cards, DEX, wallet) run 22 to 48s and explain nothing. Nobody needs a card explained. Everyone needs "sell to AI agents" explained.
2. **The goal sets the spine and the CTA.** Signups now → a gift and a comment-to-get CTA, or a campaign link. Awareness → a tagline. Availability → store badges. Credibility → "just getting started". Switching → the enemy's own UI.
3. **The audience sets the world.** Football fans and young spenders get loud play. Crypto traders get cinema and in-jokes. People trusting an app with their salary get quiet premium. Founders and operators get a founder talking to them.
4. **Trust has a direction.** The more money a product holds, the quieter the film. Plasma, a bank-like account, never shouts. Cero rewards spending and Predictstreet is a game, so they can. Slash holds money but needs explaining, so it pairs a calm founder with a luxurious, quiet material: gold on black.
5. **One brand, one world, many goals.** Plasma made six films for six goals with six spines, and every one lives in the same quiet world. The world belongs to the brand. The spine belongs to the goal.

## 2. The three filters

### Filter 1: Goal → spine

| Goal | Spine | Length | CTA | References |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Signups now, new category | Founder-led explainer: claim, "Let me show you how it works.", persona, features, enemy, gift, comment-to-get | 95-125s | Comment [keyword] | Slash, Contra, Meridian |
| Signups now, familiar category | Offer film: hook, the product loop in one take, the offer, CTA | 25-35s | Offer + link | Plasma Android, Predictstreet |
| Awareness / introduce the brand | Device film: one concept device carries it | 30-40s | Tagline | Cero |
| Available now | Announcement: void, "Now open.", how to start, life with it, open to everyone | 35-40s | Store badges | Plasma Now open |
| One feature | Proof before claim | 20-25s | Tier or feature name | Plasma Zero FX |
| Milestone | Receipts: numbers are the plot | 15-70s | "Just getting started" | Year One, $100M |
| Partnership | One sentence over many places, or a short documentary | 30-80s | Co-brand lockup | Deel, Aurora |
| Switch from an incumbent | Manifesto: their pain, morph, our verbs | 40-50s | URL | Phantom |
| Hype before launch | World reacts: payoff, time jump, origin, turn | 60-100s | "Coming" + URL | Motoswap |

### Filter 2: Audience and trust → world

A world is a closed kit: material, palette, type, sound, how cuts are timed, and which moves it allows.

| World | Material | Palette | Type | Sound | Cuts | Native moves | Forbidden |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Loud play** (Cero, Predictstreet) | 3D objects, foil, POV phone shots | White or deep color + one saturated accent | Serif or wide heavy caps | Compressed music, plateau | On action | Hero object, object carry, object as wipe, pack burst, rolling values, sunburst inversion, shape rhyme, word per beat | Long holds, small quiet type, documentary |
| **Cinematic genre** (Motoswap) | AI cinema, screen-filmed graphics | Dark + one neon | Heavy glow caps, mono for time cards | Dialogue + score, crescendo | On speech and action | Genre borrowing, texture, stat slams, overload then void, time jump, push-in, captions | Whitespace, cute 3D, store badges |
| **Quiet premium** (Plasma) | Real lifestyle footage, glass UI, silver card | White/black, no accent | Small sans, sentence case, full stop | Ambient music, drops and fades | On the beat grid, a quarter beat early | Void, word slot, UI in the world, toasts, anchor swap, one sentence many shots, carousel, bookend, receipts chart | Glitch, confetti, shouting caps, jokes |
| **Founder explainer** (Shown Media) | Founder A-roll + one cutaway material | Brand's | Captions + one display face | Voice-led, music low | On the voice | Say it see it, persona map, real UI zoom, split-flap numbers, bleeped enemy, proof stack, reply-box CTA | A second cutaway material, music-locked cutting |
| **Kinetic manifesto** (Phantom) | Bold type, brand color field, 3D props | Brand color + black | Rounded heavy sans, one word at a time | Music only | On the beat grid | Word per beat, type-through, competitor morph, verbs as features, circle masks, stickers | Faces, long sentences |

The founder explainer world is the one hybrid: its cutaways borrow from exactly one other world. Slash's gold on black is quiet premium made luxurious. Contra's paper and color leans loud play. Meridian's skit borrows the cinematic genre for 17 seconds and then never again. **One cutaway world per film, never two.**

### Filter 3: Beats → moves

Each beat in the spine has one job. Pick one move per beat, only from the world's native list.

| Job | Moves by world |
|---|---|
| **Hook** (0-3s) | Play: hero object in the real world (coin toss). Cinema: fake breaking news. Quiet: black + "Now open." or an unfinished sentence. Founder: the one-sentence claim. Manifesto: the enemy's UI multiplying |
| **Problem** | Play: the bored "before" state. Cinema: the skit. Quiet: rarely shown. Founder: bleeped incumbents. Manifesto: the enemy's worst screen |
| **Product appears** | Play: object carry into the phone. Quiet: UI in the world. Founder: "Let me show you how it works." Manifesto: competitor morph |
| **How it works** | Play: 3 step cards. Quiet: one take through the UI, or a word slot. Founder: persona walkthrough, say it see it. Manifesto: verbs, one per beat |
| **Breadth** | Play: circle collage. Quiet: anchor swap, one sentence many shots, logo storm. Founder: brand names in real UI |
| **Proof** | Play: rolling values. Cinema: stat slams on screens. Quiet: chart counter, toasts. Founder: proof stack |
| **Desire** | Play: pack burst, jackpot, inversion. Quiet: lifestyle montage. Founder: "The right [persona] could…" |
| **Turn** (optional) | Cinema: overload then void, time jump. Quiet: void. Founder: the outrageous promise or the joke |
| **CTA** | Play/Quiet: tagline or store badges. Cinema: "IS COMING" push-in. Founder: reply box with the keyword. Manifesto: URL |
| **Resolve** | All: bookend on the hero object or mark |

## 3. The budget: why the films feel clean

Count what each film leaves out.

| Film | Moves it uses | Moves it could have used but didn't |
|---|---|---|
| Cero | Hero object, carry, wipe, pack burst, rolling values, shape rhyme, inversion, bookend | Founder, enemy, void, anchor swap, glitch, documentary |
| Plasma Now open | Bookend, void, word slot, UI in world, toasts, carousel | Hero object burst, glitch, founder, jokes, enemy |
| Motoswap | News cold open, texture, stat slams, overload then void, time jump, push-in | Product UI walkthrough, store badges, whitespace |
| Slash | Founder claim, say it see it, persona map, split-flaps, bleeped enemy, joke, proof stack, reply box | Music-locked cutting, glitch, lifestyle montage, a second material |

The rules that fall out:

1. **Every move must come from the spine or from the hero object.** Cero has eight moves and still reads as one idea, because all eight are the coin or the reward. A move that serves neither is decoration. Cut it.
2. **One of each:** one spine, one world, one hero object or material, one accent color, one display typeface, one void, one joke.
3. **Pick the sync once.** With a voice, cut on the voice. Without one, cut on the grid (quiet, manifesto) or on action (loud). Never switch mid-film.
4. **One hybrid seam at most.** Meridian's skit to founder, and Slash's founder to gold cutaways, are the only seams in the set. Each is crossed once, at a clear beat.

## 4. Same product, different goals, different films

A fictional product shows the logic end to end: **loop.**, a card that rounds up every purchase into a savings vault paying 4.5% from T-bills.

Inputs: Gen Z and millennial spenders. Money held, so trust matters. Round-ups are familiar, but round-ups into a yield vault are half-new. Assets: a founder willing to talk, the real app, budget for AI-generated cutaways.

### Goal A: 5,000 signups in the launch week

- **Spine:** the half-new mechanic needs explaining, so founder-led explainer, cut short to about 75 seconds (less to explain than Slash).
- **World:** founder plus cutaways in **one material: the coin.** It holds money, so the cutaways run quiet premium (cream, a single coral, small sans), with the coin as the only loud element.
- **Hero object:** the coin. **Accent:** coral. **Sync:** on the voice. **Joke:** one. **Void:** none, because the founder is the turn.

| Time | Job | Line | Move |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-6s | Hook | "We built the first card that turns your spare change into a 4.5% savings account." | Founder claim, name lower third |
| 6-8s | Pivot | "Let me show you how it works." | Founder |
| 8-14s | Persona | "Say you're a student in Lisbon who buys a €3.60 coffee every morning." | Coin flips off a café table into the phone (object carry) |
| 14-30s | How it works | "loop. rounds it to €4. The 40 cents lands in your vault… earning 4.5%, backed by U.S. T-bills." | Real UI zoom; coin drops into a ring; split-flap flips to 4.5% |
| 30-40s | Breadth | "It works on every tap, in every country." | Anchor swap: card locked, Lisbon → Tokyo → New York |
| 40-50s | Enemy | "Banks pay you 0.01% and charge you to look at your money." | Bleeped banks, dark beat |
| 50-56s | Promise + joke | "If a round-up ever goes missing, we'll pay you back 10x and I'll personally apologise in a handwritten letter." | Founder, letter cutaway |
| 56-64s | Proof | "$4.2M saved in beta by 18,000 people." | Rolling value crosses a milestone → cream flips to coral |
| 64-70s | Gift | "Sign up this week and we'll seed your vault with $10." | Coin drops into the ring, split-flap reads $10 |
| 70-75s | CTA | "Comment loop and we'll DM you an invite." | Reply box, "loop" typed, then the coin bookend |

### Goal B: celebrate $10M saved

- **Spine:** receipts. **World:** the same quiet cream-and-coral world. The brand keeps its world, the goal changes the spine.
- **Film:** 18 seconds, one shot. "$0" rolls to "$10M" while the savings curve draws and both axes rescale. Coins drop into the line at each spike. Ends on the coin mark. This is `examples/milestone-chart` re-skinned.

The same product, the same world, two different stories, because the goals differ.

## 5. Producing at the same level

What separates these films from amateur work isn't the number of effects. It's finish on a small number of things.

| Layer | What "same level" means | How we get there |
|---|---|---|
| Script | Every line has one fact, a number or a named brand. 155-180 wpm for founders. | Template, read aloud, `tools/transcribe.py` to check the take |
| Founder A-roll | One clean setup, real workplace, good mic, eyeline just off lens | Shoot the full script 3 times |
| UI | Real product, zoomed so a phone viewer can read it, cursor eased | `/stageshow`, rendered through `/hyperframes` |
| Cutaway objects | One material, same light, same lens across every object | Generate stills with one fixed prompt prefix (material, light, background, lens), animate in HyperFrames or image-to-video |
| Type | One display face, one caption style, all the way through | Set once in the HyperFrames composition |
| Motion | Eased in and out, nothing linear except counters; shared transition carriers | `docs/visual-grammar.md` timing sheet |
| Sound | Music bed + one SFX per cut + a riser into each reveal; voice on top at -14 LUFS | Mix check with `tools/flow_report.py` |
| QC | Muted watch, contact-sheet read, rubric pass | `/creative-director` step 8 |

The fixed prompt prefix is how AI-generated cutaways stay one world. Example for the loop. material: *"single coral enamel coin with a cream ring, studio product shot, soft key light from top left, cream seamless background, 85mm, shallow depth of field, no text"*. Every object prompt starts with the same words. Only the object changes.

## 6. The decision path, in one line each

1. **What must the viewer do after watching?** → spine and CTA.
2. **Is the category new to them?** → explain (founder, 75-125s) or show (25-40s).
3. **How much money or trust does the product hold?** → quieter world the more it holds.
4. **Who is watching?** → which world.
5. **What's the product's core action as an object?** → hero object and material.
6. **For each beat of the spine, which single move from this world does the job?** → shot list.
7. **What's left over?** → cut it.

The blueprint template, `.claude/skills/creative-director/templates/video-blueprint.md`, walks through these seven questions and produces a one-page plan.
